Field & Technical Skills

Equipped with hands-on training in field techniques and laboratory diagnostics tailored for ecological conservation:

🔬 Ecological Surveying

Line transects, quadrat sampling, animal sampling techniques, and population estimation.

📸 Wildlife Monitoring

Camera trapping, bird watching programs, live trapping (rodents/bats), and safe handling techniques.

🦋 Entomology & Botany

Entomological field sampling, insect identification, and preparing herbarium sheets (e.g., Cosmos sulphureus).

🧪 Lab & Taxidermy

Taxidermy of larger mammals (fishing cat stuffing), skeleton preparation (Indian star tortoise), and mosquito larval identification.

Extensive field site exposure including Knuckles Range, Horton Plains, Trincomalee mangroves, and Mannar Wind Power Plant.
