Academic Work

Research & Projects

Ongoing · Field Project

Distribution of Mammal Species: University Premises vs Mihintale Sanctuary

A comparative study of mammal species distribution in a man-made ecosystem versus a natural habitat — BIO3207 Field Project (Jan–Jun 2026).

Group Project · Ecology

Pollinator Diversity & Abundance in Mihintale Area

Study of diversity and abundance in pollinators within two selected habitats in Mihintale and their correlation with each habitat type — BIO2302 Principles of Ecology (2024–2025).

Group Project · Animal Behaviour

Feeding Habits of Snails

Behavioural study examining feeding patterns and preferences of snails — ZOO2203 Animal Behavior (Jan–Apr 2024).

Group Project · Microbiology

Novel Food Product: "Petal Crush"

Development of a fermented drink as a novel food product, followed by a review paper on alternative protein sources and microbial applications — MIB3204 Food Microbiology (2025).

Review Paper · Environmental Pollution

Climate Change Threats to Earth's Wild Animals

Viva and analysis of research by Ripple, Wolf, Gregg & Torres-Romero on climate change impacts on global wildlife — BIO3203 (July 2025).

Field Survey · Medical Entomology

Dengue Vector Surveillance

Mosquito larval identification at Mihintale tank and paddy fields; dengue surveys at home gardens and university premises — ZOO3201 (2025).
