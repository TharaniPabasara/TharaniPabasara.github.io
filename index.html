Research & Academic Projects

A summary of research projects and technical evaluations undertaken during my undergraduate tenure:

Comparative Mammal Distribution Study (BIO3207)

Research: Distribution of Mammal Species in a Man-Made Ecosystem and a Natural Habitat: A Comparative Study of University Premises and Mihintale Sanctuary.

Pollinator Abundance & Diversity (BIO2302)

Group Project: Study of Diversity and Abundance in Pollinators within Two Selected Habitats in Mihintale Area and Their Correlation with Habitat Profiles.

Environmental Impact Assessments (BDC3202)

Field environmental impact assessment visits and logging tracking metrics for the Mannar wind power plant and the Yan Oya reservoir.

Food Microbiology & Product Innovation (MIB3204)

Novel Product: "Petal Crush" (a fermented drink group project). Authored a comprehensive evaluation paper on alternative protein profiles and microbial applications.
