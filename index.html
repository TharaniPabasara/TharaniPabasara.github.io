Field & Technical Skills

Equipped with hands-on training in field techniques and laboratory diagnostics tailored for ecological conservation:

🔬 Ecological Surveying

Line transects, quadrat sampling, animal sampling techniques, and population estimation.

📸 Wildlife Monitoring

Camera trapping, bird watching programs, live trapping (rodents/bats), and safe handling techniques.

🦋 Entomology & Botany

Entomological field sampling, insect identification, and preparing herbarium sheets.

🧪 Lab & Taxidermy

Taxidermy of larger mammals (fishing cat stuffing), skeleton preparation (Indian star tortoise), and mosquito larval identification.

🗺️ Field Familiarity

Extensive field site exposure including Knuckles Range, Horton Plains, Trincomalee mangroves, and Mannar Wind Power Plant.

Research & Academic Projects

A summary of research projects, field presentations, and technical evaluations undertaken during my undergraduate tenure:

Comparative Mammal Distribution Study (BIO3207)

Research: Distribution of Mammal Species in a Man-Made Ecosystem and a Natural Habitat: A Comparative Study of University Premises and Mihintale Sanctuary.

Pollinator Abundance & Diversity (BIO2302)

Group Project: Study of Diversity and Abundance in Pollinators within Two Selected Habitats in Mihintale Area and Their Correlation with Each Habitat Type.

Environmental Impact & Risk Assessment (BDC3202)

Field impact study visits to the Mannar wind power plant and the Yan Oya reservoir.

Food Microbiology & Product Innovation (MIB3204)

Novel Product Invention: "Petal Crush" (a fermented drink group project). Authored a review paper on alternative protein sources and microbial applications.
